## No guarantees

- A **high score does not mean a token is safe.** Tokens with clean on-chain metrics rug every day. Scammers actively engineer tokens to pass automated checks.
- A **flag does not mean a token will fail.** Flagged tokens can and do pump.
- All heuristics (wash-trading detection, tribute detection, bundle detection, sniper estimation, and others) are **approximations** with false positives and false negatives.
- Data comes from third-party public APIs (DexScreener, RugCheck, Helius, Jupiter, GeckoTerminal, GoPlus). It can be **wrong, stale, incomplete, or unavailable** at any time. This project has no affiliation with, and no control over, any of these services.

## Memecoins are a high-risk environment

Memecoins are among the most speculative assets in existence. Most go to zero. Total loss of funds is a normal, frequent outcome — not an edge case. Only ever use money you can afford to lose entirely.

## DLMM / liquidity provision carries its own risks

This tool is oriented toward finding candidates for DLMM liquidity positions. Providing liquidity adds risks **on top of** token risk, including divergence loss (impermanent loss): on a volatile or collapsing token, an LP position can lose substantial value **even without a rug**, and a position left out of range earns nothing. Passing this scanner's checks does not protect a liquidity position in any way.
